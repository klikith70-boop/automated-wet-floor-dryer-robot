# Mechanical Frame Specifications

## Structure
* **Material:** Welded mild-steel rectangular hollow section tubing.
* **Layout:** Dual-tier chassis housing power, actuation, drying, and computational layers.

## Subsystem Layout
* **Lower Deck:** Houses the 12V Amardeep battery, differential drive DC motors, and front-facing absorbent cylindrical roller.
* **Upper Deck:** Houses the Arduino Uno R3, dual L293D drivers, 12V SPDT relay module, and high-velocity thermal blower.
* **Sensory Front:** Forward-facing infrared obstacle sensor positioned low to identify obstructions and boundary walls.
